##🌐ABOUT JOBFusion

> 💡 **JOB Fusion** is a modern job-search platform that brings
> opportunities from multiple companies together in one place.

It helps students and job seekers:

- 🔎 Find suitable job opportunities
- 🏢 Explore multiple companies
- 🎯 Search according to skills
- ⚡ Access jobs quickly
- 🔗 Apply through official company websites
- 📱 Use the platform on mobile and desktop
